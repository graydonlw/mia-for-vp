QR sheet

Everything here points at https://mia-for-vp.vercel.app. Print this page to PDF and the poster lands on its own sheet, with the cut-outs after it. The codes are error-correction level H, so a scuff or a thumb over one corner still scans.

Sizes are deliberate: the poster code is 60mm, which scans from roughly two metres; the cut-outs are 30mm, for arm's length. Below about 25mm on paper it stops being reliable.

Raw files, if you want them in Canva or a slide instead: brand/assets/qr/mia-qr.svg (vector, any size), mia-qr.png (1640px), mia-qr-on-shell.svg (for a non-white background).
